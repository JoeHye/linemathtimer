Step 1 — Clock a baseline

Don't guess the "average time per person" — clock it. Press Start when the person currently being served begins, then hit Lap the moment each person finishes and the next one steps up. Time at least 3 people before trusting the average — one person tells you almost nothing, since any single transaction could be unusually fast or slow.
